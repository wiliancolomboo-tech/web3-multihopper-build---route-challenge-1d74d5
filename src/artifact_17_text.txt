================================================================================
                    MULTIHOPPER PROTOCOL OPERATIONAL RUNBOOK
================================================================================

1. INCIDENT: SLIPPAGE SPIKE / EXCESS REVERTS (>5% in 5 min)
   - Trigger: Discord alert or Prometheus alert: 'MultiHopperHighRevertRate'
   - Action: 
     a. Query daemon logs: `docker logs multihopper-route-worker --tail 200`
     b. Verify whether price divergence originates from high-volatility pool (e.g. DLMM bin shift).
     c. Dynamically scale the minimum out buffer parameter in the routing daemon client config.

2. RUNTIME REVENUE AUDIT & RECONCILIATION
   - Command: `anchor run audit-integrator --integrator-id <ID>`
   - Verifies on-chain accumulated revenue PDAs against internal accounting ledgers.
   - Sweep integrator fees: Execute authorized claim instruction to payout destinations.

3. ROTATION OF PROTOCOL AUTHORITY KEYS
   - Uses multi-sig Squads v3 interface.
   - Transfer protocol authority via Anchor:
     `multihopper_engine::set_authority(new_authority_multisig_pda)`
